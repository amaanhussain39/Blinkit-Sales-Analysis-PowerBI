 🟡 Blinkit Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI Project)

🔍  Objective

This dashboard analyzes **Blinkit's outlet performance**, focusing on **sales, outlet size, item types, and customer ratings** to identify which factors contribute most to business growth and profitability.

---

 📊 Key Metrics Overview

| Metric                        | Value      | Insight                                    |
| :---------------------------- | :--------- | :----------------------------------------- |
| 💰 Total Sales                |   $1.20M   | Overall company sales across all outlets   |
| 📦   No. of Items Sold        |    9K      | Total number of unique products/items sold |
| 💵   Average Sales per Item   |   $141     | Indicates moderate revenue per item        |
| 🌟   Average Rating           |   4 / 5    | Reflects positive customer experience      |

---

  🧠  Deep Insights

### 🏪 1. Outlet Performance by Type

* **Supermarket Type 1** leads with **$787.55K total sales** and **5,577 items sold**, showing it’s the **primary revenue driver**.
* **Grocery Store** and **Supermarket Type 2 & 3** perform moderately but have **similar ratings (4/5)** — indicating consistent customer satisfaction.

**💡 Insight:**
Focus future marketing and supply chain efforts on **Supermarket Type 1**, as it contributes the **highest share of sales (≈65%)**.

---

### 📍 2. Outlet Location Analysis

* **Tier 3 locations** lead with **$472.13K in sales**, outperforming Tier 2 and Tier 1.
* Despite being smaller cities, Tier 3 outlets show **stronger customer engagement and sales per outlet**.

**💡 Insight:**
Blinkit’s demand is growing fastest in **Tier 3 cities**, suggesting **potential expansion opportunities** in rural and semi-urban markets.

---

### 📊 3. Outlet Size Contribution

* **Medium-sized outlets** contribute the most: **$444.79K** of total sales.
* Small and high-size outlets show smaller contributions (**$248.99K** and **$507.90K** combined).

**💡 Insight:**
Medium outlets are the **sweet spot for efficiency** — balancing inventory, cost, and performance effectively.

---

### 🍎 4. Product Category Insights

Top 5 Selling Categories:

1. **Fruits & Vegetables** — $0.18M
2. **Snack Foods** — $0.18M
3. **Household Items** — $0.14M
4. **Frozen Foods** — $0.12M
5. **Dairy Products** — $0.10M

**💡 Insight:**
Blinkit’s top-performing categories are **fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG)** — essential for daily needs.
👉 Focus on **stock optimization** and **dynamic pricing** in these high-demand categories.

---

### 🧈 5. Fat Content Analysis

* **Low Fat products** generate **slightly higher sales ($0.31M)** compared to **Regular ($0.25M)**.
* Indicates a **growing health-conscious customer base**.

**💡 Insight:**
Increase visibility and promotions for **Low Fat and Healthy Alternatives** — aligns with customer trends.

---

### ⏳ 6. Outlet Establishment Trend

* Sales steadily increased from **$78K (2010)** to a peak of **$205K (2018)**, followed by stabilization (~$131K in 2022).

**💡 Insight:**
Post-2018, Blinkit reached **market maturity** — future growth depends on **innovation and diversification** rather than expansion alone.
